FIFI FALLS

The dark keeps her.

Look: the mouse, after one click on the view (Esc lets go). The hands sway as you look. Move: ↑ ↓ or W S walk; ← → or A D strafe. Mouse: left click strikes, hold left for the class power right click fires (hold to draw the bow). Tap the wheel to crouch (slower, quieter, advantage on Stealth); hold the wheel to hide (rogues). 1–9: action cards (click uses the lit one). Space: jump. E: forage, open chests, pick up. M: map. 0: leap back out of reach (rogues, rangers and fighters).
